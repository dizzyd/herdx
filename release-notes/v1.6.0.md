## A mute switch within reach

A small speaker at the foot of the sidebar now mutes the agent sounds, and
unmutes them again — no trip to Settings when you are on a call or just want
quiet for a while.

It is the same switch as **Settings ▸ Sounds**, not a second one, so the two
always agree and the choice is remembered across launches. The glyph is drawn
faint while sounds are on and a shade stronger while muted, since a mute you
have forgotten about is the one that costs you an agent's question.
