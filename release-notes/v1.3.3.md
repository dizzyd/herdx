## The agent sounds stay quiet while the screen is locked

An agent finishing, or blocking on a question, made its noise whether or not
you were at the machine — so a locked Mac announced your work to whoever was in
the room, and kept announcing it all the while you were away from it.

HerdX now asks macOS whether the screen is locked each time a sound is due, and
says nothing while it is. Nothing is saved up: unlocking does not play back what
happened without you, and the next thing an agent does is audible again.

## Coming from 1.3.0

1.3.1 and 1.3.2 were published and then went missing for a day — their tags
were deleted by a repository mirror, which turns a release on GitHub into a
draft without saying so, and left 1.3.0 looking like the newest version to
anybody who asked. Both are back where they belong. If HerdX never mentioned
them to you, that is why, and their notes are worth a look: **1.3.1** fixes
remote machines refusing to connect when HerdX is opened from the Finder, and
**1.3.2** brings a theme for light and one for dark, a window that stops taking
its colours from whatever a pane is painting, and a tidier Settings.
